7  THE HANDOFF -- what the formula-first derivation gets
==========================================================================
  * the MEASURAND: P(successor arrives by t | hole outstanding), timed
    from DETECTION (the gap_data trigger), at the receiver, per hole.
  * its SHAPE: section 2's per-cell quantiles by outcome.
  * its ANCHOR: section 3's crossing point -- the false-repair boundary
    in time -- with its own thin/absent verdicts intact.
  * whether the formula must CARRY THE DIAL: section 4.
  * whether the estimate is STABLE ENOUGH TO BUILD ON: section 5.
  * WHAT REMAINS UNMEASURED, and it is NOT nothing: every duration here
    excludes creation-to-detection (not receiver-observable); `orig`
    cannot separate a late reorder from a retransmit; the `open` census
    is right-censored by the harness SIGKILL; and one seed was run.
