## 5 — CLAUSE B: beta vs THE DELIVERED-LATENCY PROBE, LIKE-FOR-LIKE.
##     **B CAN REJECT. B CANNOT ACQUIT.** A candidate inside the band is
##     recorded as NOT-SHOWN-BIASED, never as unbiased.

  THE PROBE'S OWN LIMITS, RESTATED BEFORE ITS NUMBERS:
   1. SITE. It measures the peer path and excludes sender scheduling,
      store residency and the ack-generation path — all of which ADD
      dispersion. Its dispersion is a LOWER bound on the ack path's, so
      every beta below is a LOWER bound on the true bias.
   2. CENSORING. A lost probe never produces a sample and the losses
      come from exactly the worst states. censor_frac prints beside
      every functional; >20% kills the leg (latt_probe contract bar),
      and P90 dies structurally above 10% (0.90 > 1-c).
   3. SAMPLING RATE, AND IT IS SPECIFIC TO `msd`. The probe runs at
      20 Hz; the sender's RTT stream runs at kHz. `msd` is MEASURABLY
      rate-dependent, so beta_msd against this probe is NOT like-for-
      like in the one axis `msd` is known to depend on. It is printed
      and it is marked CONFOUNDED; it is not read as a pass or a fail.
   4. rvar reads against `sd` LITERALLY, per §4's 'moment-class
      candidateS'. rvar estimates E|dev| = 0.7979*sigma for a Gaussian,
      so beta_rvar carries a built-in ~0.80x. DISCLOSED, NOT CORRECTED.
